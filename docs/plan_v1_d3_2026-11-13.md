# Plan: score the v1 warning signs on WearGait-PD (13 Nov 2026)

Written before any WearGait-PD v1 number exists. To be approved, committed and pushed before anything runs.

## Why
The v1 ablation (table T11: do the conference's warning signs add anything once witnesses and MI are in?) was
scored on UTD-MHAD and MMAct only. On WearGait-PD the signs themselves were computed in full on 12 Nov (queue I,
`signals_d3_all_s0-1-2.parquet`, 10 folds x 3 seeds, "ALL DONE I 14:48"), but never scored, because
`cert_v1_score.py` lists sensors for d1v2 and d2v only. Rule (13 Nov): no planned experiment is skipped.

## What changes
1. `scripts/cert_v1_score.py`: one edit, the WearGait-PD entry in its two sensor lists, taken from
   `cert_signals.py` (d3: sensors arms, trunk, legs; all three are IMUs, so all three get harmless changes):
   `PHYS["d3"] = ["arms", "trunk", "legs"]`, `MODS["d3"] = ["arms", "trunk", "legs"]`. Nothing else.
2. Nothing else is re-trained or re-computed; the stored signs, experts and MI outputs are used as they are.

## Order
- V1. After the edit, re-run the scorer on d1v2 and d2v and compare with the frozen files
  (`scripts/compare_outputs.py`): must be byte-identical (the edit must not touch the other datasets).
- V2. Smoke run on the fold-0 seed-0 files (`--tag f0_s0`): code check only, never overwrites full-run files.
- V3. Full run: `python scripts/cert_v1_score.py --dataset d3`. Its own checks must pass: signs and experts files
  agree on the fused answer; C3's lie AUROC equal in the two scorers (1e-9); REV3 equal to the frozen
  `mi_d3_per_subject.csv` (1e-9).
- V4. Re-freeze: table T11 gains WearGait-PD (table script); fingerprint re-written (the files frozen this morning
  must keep their checksums; only v1_d3 files are added); new tag `journal-results-frozen-v2`; the
  reproducibility check re-run at the new tag, now including the v1 d3 scoring. The first tag stays as it is.

## Predictions (from the pattern on UTD-MHAD and MMAct; unit = fold, 10 folds; revision 1)
- P1. The V1 system (confidence + S1 + S2) catches lies worse than REV3: REV3 minus V1 lie AUROC > 0 in at least
  9 of 10 folds, and its mean interval above 0.
- P2. Adding the v1 signs to REV3 changes almost nothing: REV3 + V1 minus REV3 lie AUROC within +/-0.005 (mean).
- P3. Each v1 sign on its own (S1 stability drop, S1 stability flip, S2 hide-one flips, S2 hide-one shift) has a
  lower lie AUROC than the best single witness sign ("X lowest expert support").
- P4. V1 names the lying sensor much worse than REV3: REV3 minus V1 naming at least 20 points.
A prediction that fails is reported as failed; nothing is re-tuned after seeing the result.
