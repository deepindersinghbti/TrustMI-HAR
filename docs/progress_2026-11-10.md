   on 6 Nov; nothing had been tested on the journal datasets yet.
2. **Test scripts taught the journal datasets** (`eval_operators.py`, `cert_signals.py`, `cert_experts.py`,
   `cert_mi.py`, `cert_evidential.py`). Lies are now planted in every sensor of both datasets.
   - Problem found: `cert_mi.py` time-stretched the reference clips of every sensor, including video. A video is
     4 fixed tokens, so this crashed on MMAct with video. Fix: only watch and phone are stretched (as the training
     script already did). A test was added that fails on the old line and passes on the new one.
   - Check: fold 0 of both conference datasets re-run and compared row by row with the stored files: identical
     (largest difference 0) for witnesses, MI and the v1 signs (`scripts/check_conference_unchanged.sh`).
3. **Queue C** (`scripts/run_journal_queue_c.sh`): lie tests, witnesses, per-clip MI (own and frozen), TMC / ECML,
   witnesses on TMC / ECML, v1 signs. One-fold smoke run first, then the full run: 51 minutes, no failed step,
   every stored prediction reproduced (largest logit difference 0.125, limit 0.5).
4. **Backups chosen on validation** (`scripts/choose_backups_journal.py`), same rule as in September (the fused
   model without the sensor must beat the best single-sensor model by more than 1 point on validation):
   - UTD-MHAD: drop IMU -> skeleton model; drop skeleton -> IMU model (+0.6 points only, so the simpler model);
     drop video -> IMU + skeleton model.
   - MMAct: all three use the two-sensor fused model (gains of 3.2 to 6.1 points on validation).
5. **Scoring** (`cert_mi_score.py --dataset d1v2 | d2v`). The conference numbers were re-checked first and
   reproduce to 1e-16. For the journal datasets the certificate numbers equal an independent computation by
   `cert_policy.py` exactly.

|---|---|---|

## 3. Weak points, stated plainly
- **Cost on normal clips.** The use / drop policy costs 1 to 4 points on normal clips (revision 1). Revision 2
  (drops capped at 5% of normal validation clips; post hoc, labelled) lowers the cost to about 0.5 to 2 points.
- **Natural mistakes.** On MMAct, TMC spots its own natural mistakes better than REV3 spots the fused model's
  (error AUROC 0.889 vs 0.829). MI alone is at chance there (0.511): sensors that belong together say nothing
  about whether the answer is right. This is the "high mutual information is not enough" point, in numbers.
- **Abstention in revision 1.** Adding MI lowers "answer the safest 80%" accuracy on MMAct by 3.4 points (0/20);
  in revision 2 it raises it by 2.0 (19/20).
- **MI does not improve naming on UTD-MHAD** (-0.5, not clear); on MMAct it adds +1.4 [+0.6, +2.1].
- **Frozen-encoder MI is weaker** than the own-encoder version on both datasets (MMAct 0.881 vs 0.930).
- The same-action swap lowers watch-phone MI a little on MMAct (1.67 -> 1.41 / 1.27; a lie gives about -3.3),
  as in the conference runs.
