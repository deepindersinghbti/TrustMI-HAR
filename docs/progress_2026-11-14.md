## 2. Two decisions in the plan, confirmed when the plan was approved
- **D1:** no "frozen last value" on the 247 real no-watch clips, because the watch was never recorded there and
  there is no last value to freeze. The stuck watch is tested instead as the stuck fault of Addition 1, on normal
  MMAct clips.
- **D2:** saturation uses the standard full-scale ranges of MEMS motion sensors: level 1 +/-8 g and +/-1000 deg/s,
  level 2 +/-4 g and +/-500 deg/s, level 3 +/-2 g and +/-250 deg/s.

## 3. A wording correction in the plan (before any Addition 1 code ran)
The plan first said the stuck fault starts at "a random onset". A frozen stretch that must reach the last sample and
has a fixed length (25 / 50 / 100% of the clip) can start at only one place (75 / 50 / 0% of the clip), so there is
no random draw: the fault is deterministic. The plan was corrected and the correction is marked in it with its date.

### 5.1 Addition 3: the dead watch as raw zeros (MMAct, 247 clips, 6 people, 3 seeds pooled; table T14)
| System | Accuracy alone | After use / drop | Flagged | Names the watch (of flagged) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Doing nothing (main model) | 68.7% | – | – | – |
| **REV3 (main)** | 68.7% | **53.6%** | 90.4% | 45.7% |
| C3 | 68.7% | 50.7% | 87.2% | 36.1% |
| TMC alone | 85.0% | 85.0% | 2.4% | 44.4% |
| ECML alone | 82.3% | 81.0% | 21.3% | 49.4% |
| Backup, watch known missing | – | 84.1% | – | – |

Predictions: N1, N2 and N3 **not confirmed**; N4 confirmed.
- N1 (doing nothing worse with raw zeros than with the mean fill, 55.9%): it is **better**, 68.7%.
- N2 (REV3 flags more and names the watch more often than with the mean fill): flags 90.4% vs 91.4%, names the
  watch on 45.7% vs 48.7%. Both slightly lower.
- N3 (REV3's use / drop beats doing nothing): **53.6% vs 68.7%**, clearly worse. Driven by person 2 (126 of the
  247 clips): 31.5% vs 70.6%. Mean of the four people with 28 or more clips: 63.4% vs 67.0%.
- N4 (detection at least as good as with the mean fill, 0.945): 0.949. Confirmed.
- Reading: REV3 still spots these clips very well, but when it drops it often drops the wrong sensor (the watch
  is named on fewer than half of the flagged clips), so its answer is worse than keeping the main model's. TMC and
  ECML are much more robust to a dead watch here. This joins the weakness noted on 12 Nov (naming a silent sensor)
  as a limitation for the paper.

### 5.2 Addition 2: IMUs off for part of the clip (WearGait-PD, 10 folds; tables T15, figure F4)
Legs, all 8 IMUs off, REV3 minus doing nothing (points): p = 25% -0.21; 50% +1.06; 75% +7.85; whole clip
(stored) +39.5. Doing nothing at p = 25%: 95.6% (whole clip 52.5%). REV3 detection AUROC: 0.647, 0.730, 0.824,
0.912. With one IMU off, every group and every p: -0.17 to -0.64 points.

Predictions I1-I4: all four **confirmed**.
- The gain grows with the share of the clip that is off; at 25% the model copes on its own and REV3's drop costs a
  little; from 6 leg IMUs at 75% REV3 helps in 10 of 10 folds.
- Reading: the 12 Nov explanation holds in time as well as across IMUs: REV3 pays off when most of a group is
  gone for most of the clip, and costs a little otherwise. This also fits the real dropouts (partial in both ways).

### 5.3 Addition 1: stuck sensor and saturation (all three datasets; tables T13-T13d)
Harmful-fault AUROC (new faults pooled, revision 1):

| Dataset | Confidence | REV3 | TMC | REV3 names the faulty sensor | TMC names it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UTD-MHAD | **0.922** | 0.900 | 0.881 | 70.5% | 17.0% |
| MMAct | **0.883** | 0.876 | 0.848 | 66.3% | 11.4% |
| WearGait-PD | 0.909 | **0.941** | 0.930 | 82.0% | 56.1% |

Predictions:
- F1 (confidence beats REV3 on every dataset): confirmed on UTD-MHAD and MMAct, **not confirmed on WearGait-PD**,
  where REV3 is better (0.941 vs 0.909). Most of this comes from a fully stuck sensor (level 3: REV3 0.968 vs
  confidence 0.898).
- F2 (REV3 names the faulty sensor more often than TMC): confirmed on all three.
- F3 (harm grows with the level): confirmed on all three, for both faults.
- F4 (saturation level 1 changes fewer than 5% of answers): confirmed on UTD-MHAD (1.49%) and WearGait-PD
  (0.00%), **not confirmed on MMAct (5.13%)**. The over-exposed video (x1.5) alone changes 15.4% of answers there;
  the motion sensors almost nothing.
- How much saturation really clips: at level 1 at most 0.004% of readings; at level 3 up to 3.1% of readings
  (UTD-MHAD IMU, where 77% of clips have some clipping). On WearGait-PD the first two levels clip almost nothing,
  so they change almost no answers (table T13d).
